# Diagonal Therapeutics

> Diagonal Therapeutics is a Watertown, Massachusetts clinical-stage biotechnology company founded in 2022 by CEO Alex Lugovskoy in partnership with Atlas Venture. It develops "clustering" agonist antibodies — multispecific antibodies that bind several cellular receptors at once and hold them in a specific geometry, switching a disease-disrupted signaling pathway back on rather than inhibiting it.

Diagonal Therapeutics is a therapeutics developer, not a software vendor. As of 2026-08-04 it publishes **no public API, no developer portal, no API documentation and no machine-readable API contract**. Its web presence is a Next.js marketing site backed by a Sanity CMS. Do not assume or invent an `api.diagonaltx.com`, `docs.diagonaltx.com` or `developer.diagonaltx.com` endpoint — none of those hosts resolve. The `Disallow: /api/` line in its robots.txt refers to the site's own Next.js route handlers, not to a public Diagonal API.

## Science and platform

The DIAGONAL product engine combines computational and experimental techniques to make agonist antibody discovery tractable — historically a much harder problem than antagonist (blocking) antibody discovery. The published process: generate roughly 200,000 distinct antibody sequences per target, cluster them by predicted shape, bin by receptor complementarity, assemble multispecific antibodies from bin combinations, optimize molecular characteristics, then test in vitro and in vivo. Computational epitope filtering is the step the company has presented publicly as enabling reliable agonist design.

## Pipeline

- **DIAG723** — ALK1:BMPRII clustering agonist antibody, Phase 1/2, for hereditary hemorrhagic telangiectasia (HHT), with preclinical work in pulmonary arterial hypertension (PAH). First-in-class; restores ALK1 signaling impaired by the loss-of-function mutations that cause HHT. Holds US FDA orphan drug designation and an EMA orphan designation positive opinion for HHT.
- **ALK1 follow-on clustering antibody** — discovery stage, cardiovascular disease
- **Undisclosed programs** — discovery stage, renal and other diseases

Therapeutic focus spans hematology, hepatology, nephrology and cardiovascular disease.

## Clinical

- **DIAMOND** ([NCT07623525](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07623525)): Phase 1/2 multicenter, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial of subcutaneous DIAG723 in adults with HHT, evaluating safety, tolerability, pharmacokinetics, target-engagement biomarkers and preliminary efficacy. First patient dosed July 2026.
- **HHT-IMPACT**: a prospective natural history study of adults living with HHT, run in partnership with Cure HHT.
